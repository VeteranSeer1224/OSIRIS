# Project OSIRIS - Ethical Constraints & Scope Acknowledgment

By contributing to or operating this pipeline, all team members agree to the following constraints:

1. **Authorized Targets Only:** All test targets must be infrastructure owned by the project team, explicitly designated public test sandboxes, or public figures who have consented to OSINT-methodology research.
2. **No Unconsenting Private Targets:** No real, non-consenting private individual is to be targeted, profiled, or scored at any point during development, testing, or demonstration.
3. **Respect Rules of Engagement:** All data ingestion must respect `robots.txt`, platform terms of service, and rate limits. No credential-based or authentication-bypassing collection is permitted.
4. **Mandatory Explainability:** The XAI audit layer (Stage 4) is a mandatory gate. No high-risk finding may reach the final report without an attached feature attribution and fairness check.
